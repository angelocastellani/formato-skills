# The review

Fresh eyes every round. Look at the sheet, the first two seconds and the numbers, and watch the film if you can. Score
out of ten and be harsh: a 7 is not done.

1. **Stranger test.** Sound off, first two seconds: would a stranger stop? What did they think it was about?
2. **One sentence.** Say what the film was about in one sentence. If you cannot, it is two films.
3. **The hero.** Is there one? Does it change on screen, or does the picture just swap?
4. **Word and picture.** Pick five key words. Does the thing they name land on them, within a tenth of a second?
5. **Dead air.** Any stretch where nothing happens? (`bin/check.mjs` numbers, then your eyes.)
6. **Chain.** Could two sentences swap? Then it is a list.
7. **The button.** Is the last line short, dry and earned?
8. **Truth.** Every claim in `SOURCES.md`? Anything stated harder than the source?
9. **Look.** Does it hold the look's rules? Text legible on a phone? Nothing in the last 220 px? Anything that reads as a
   brand, a game or a famous cartoon?

Verify a finding by looking at the frame before changing anything. Fix the thing named, re-render only what it touches.
Numbers are diagnostics: they catch a dead film, they never approve one.
